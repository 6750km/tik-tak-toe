Tic Tac Toe Easy Go

How can we help?

The game is free and works without an account. Use the guide below or contact us if you still need help.

Sign in

Use the email and password chosen when you created the account. Confirm your email first if the app asks you to.

Reset a password

Tap Forgot password?, enter the same email, then open the newest reset email on the iPhone where the app is installed.

No email arrived

Check Spam and Promotions, wait a few minutes, and request one new email. Older links may expire after a newer request.

Delete an account

Open Account in the app, choose Delete account, read the warning, and confirm permanent deletion.
