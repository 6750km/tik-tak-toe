## App Review notes

The app is fully usable without an account. Optional email/password accounts provide profile features. There are no ads, purchases, subscriptions, or game limits in version 1.0.

To test optional account features, open Account and choose Create account. Confirm the email, then sign in. Password recovery is available from Account via Forgot password?. Profile photo upload and sign out are also available in Account.

Permanent account deletion is available at Account → Delete account → confirm. It removes the Supabase Auth user, profile, linked usage counters and avatar files. After deletion, the app clears the local session and returns to the signed-out Account screen.

Support and Privacy Policy links are available in Account and do not require sign-in.
